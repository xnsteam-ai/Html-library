Frequently asked questions

Can't find it here? Ask the team.

Can I migrate an existing pipeline?

Yes. Point us at the repository and we read your existing config, then show a dry run of what would change before anything is applied.

What counts as a build minute?

Wall-clock time a runner spends executing your build command. Queue time and cache restoration are not billed.

Do preview URLs expire?

They live as long as the pull request does, then are removed on merge or close. Pinning a preview keeps it indefinitely.

Is there a self-hosted option?

On the Enterprise plan you can run the build agents inside your own network while the control plane stays managed.

How do I cancel?

From billing settings, in one click. Your projects keep serving until the end of the period you already paid for.
